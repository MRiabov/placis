# public.footer.centered_social_nav

## Purpose

Use for source sites with a quiet centered footer: social icons or text icons, small uppercase nav,
legal links, and a copyright line. It is a good fit for institutional/portfolio sites where the
footer should not become a dense service sitemap.

## Best Fit

Good for image-led brand sites, commercial contractors, studios, venues, and references where the
footer is a light closing element rather than a conversion panel.

## Avoid

Avoid for local-service pages that need phone-first footer conversion, dense service-area links, or
emergency callout controls.

## Key Props

Use `links`, `legal_links`, `social_links`, optional `logo` or `logo_url`, `contact_line`, and
`copyright`.

## Style And Composition

The style preset owns icon treatment, centered spacing, uppercase nav labels, background color, and
footer density.
