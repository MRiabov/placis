# public.footer.logo_nav

## Purpose

Use this footer for simple logo, navigation, and legal-line endings, including
required public registration or VAT identifiers when available.

## Best Fit

Good for design-led pages or templates where the footer should stay quiet and
minimal.

## Avoid

Avoid it when contact details, service links, badges, or hours are important
conversion/support content.

## Key Props

Use logo/business identity, navigation links, legal text, legal disclosure
lines, company registration number, registered office, and VAT number where
available/applicable.

Generated sites should not drop legal disclosure identifiers just because the
footer is visually minimal. VAT IDs are conditional and should only render when
the business is VAT-registered or has confirmed VAT-taxable activity.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control logo size, link spacing, background, and text
contrast. Keep it visually subordinate to the page content.
