# public.footer.standard

## Purpose

Use this footer for standard public-site navigation, contact details, service
links, legal copy, legal disclosure identifiers, and optional badges.

## Best Fit

Good for most business websites that need practical footer navigation and
business contact details.

## Avoid

Avoid it for very minimal portfolio pages where a logo-only footer is more
appropriate.

## Key Props

Use business name, logo, links, service links, hours, address, phone, email,
badges, legal copy, legal disclosure lines, company registration number,
registered office, and VAT number where available/applicable.

Generated sites should populate company registration numbers and VAT IDs from
tenant facts when available. VAT IDs are conditional and should only render when
the business is VAT-registered or has confirmed VAT-taxable activity.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control footer background, column rhythm, link contrast,
badge sizing, and mobile stacking.
