# public.content.intro

## Purpose

Use this component for a centered introduction, section opening, or short
positioning statement.

## Best Fit

Good after a hero or before services when the page needs a concise explanation
of who the contractor serves and why the section matters.

## Avoid

Avoid it for long-form story content or image-led explanation. Use split or
story components instead.

## Key Props

Use `eyebrow`, `title`, and `paragraphs`. Keep paragraphs short and
source-backed when factual claims are made.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control type scale, measure, spacing, and muted text
color. It pairs well with services, proof bars, and galleries.
