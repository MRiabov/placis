# public.contact.panel

## Purpose

Use this component to show contact details, service area, opening hours, and contact CTAs.

## Best Fit

Good on contact pages, home page closing sections, and service pages where practical details matter.

## Avoid

Avoid it when the page only needs a short CTA band or when contact details are unverified.

## Key Props

Use business name, address, phone, email, hours, service area, links, and optional map/display settings.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control panel surface, icon/link treatment, spacing, and map/list balance. Pair it with lead forms or footer navigation.
