# public.contact.form_location

## Purpose

Use for contact pages that combine an address/map area with a CMS-managed contact form. It is useful
for contractor, venue, clinic, school, and office sites where physical location is part of the
conversion path.

## Best Fit

Good for source pages that show a map or office location beside a compact general enquiry form.

## Avoid

Do not embed third-party form providers. The form must submit through CMS/CRM form handling.

## Key Props

Use `form_id`, `fields[]`, `title`, optional `body`, `business_name`, `address`, `phone`,
`license_number`, and `map_embed_url`.

## Style And Composition

The style preset owns the map/form grid, form control styling, intro spacing, and mobile stacking.
