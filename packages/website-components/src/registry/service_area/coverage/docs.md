# public.service_area.coverage

## Purpose

Use this component to explain where a contractor works and how coverage affects
the service.

## Best Fit

Good for local trades where city, county, radius, or excluded areas matter to
lead quality.

## Avoid

Avoid it when the service area is uncertain or unverified. Keep imported
coverage claims under review until approved.

## Key Props

Use locations, radius, supporting copy, and optional map/list presentation
fields.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control map/list treatment, density, borders, and muted
copy. Place it near contact, lead form, or service sections.
