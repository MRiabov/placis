# public.privacy.notice

## Purpose

Use this component for public-form privacy notices and data-use explanations.

## Best Fit

Good near lead forms or contact pages where visitors submit personal details.

## Avoid

Avoid using it as a full legal privacy policy page unless the content is intentionally short and reviewed.

## Key Props

Use notice version, policy URL, contact email, and short privacy copy.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control readable small text, links, spacing, and contrast. Keep it clear rather than promotional.
