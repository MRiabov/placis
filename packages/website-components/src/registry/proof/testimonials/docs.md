# public.proof.testimonials

## Purpose

Use this component to show short customer testimonials or reviews with optional rating and source markers.

## Best Fit

Good for readable social proof where individual quotes matter more than one large featured review.

## Avoid

Avoid it for unsourced ratings, fabricated reviews, or dense carousel-only layouts that hide important proof.

## Key Props

Use `items[]` or `testimonials[]` with quote/body, name/author, optional rating, `source_label`, and `source_url`.

## Style And Composition

The style preset should control card surface, star color, source marker treatment, spacing, and readable contrast. Place it near FAQ, gallery, or contact CTA sections.
